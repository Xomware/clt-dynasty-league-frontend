PROPOSAL

Reverse-HPP Draft Order

Rule #57 proposes that the upcoming rookie draft order be determined by each team's Highest Possible Points (HPP) — the maximum score their roster could have achieved each week if they had set the optimal lineup.

  • Non-playoff teams are ranked ascending by season HPP — teams that left the most points on the bench pick earliest.
  • Playoff teams are parked at the back of the draft, ordered by playoff finish (worst finish picks first).
  • This rule rewards managers who maximized their roster every week and discourages lineup tanking.

This is a proposed rule only — not yet in effect. The order shown is a projection based on the current season's data.
